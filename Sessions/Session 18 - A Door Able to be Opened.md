# Key Events
- We spend a long time trying to solve the door puzzle
- The crystal pedestal has triple Sylvan letters, staring at one part, Wren starts feeling pain and fear and loss, like a diary of one who died before they should have and almost became what they were afraid of - they are concepts
- Placing different orbs on different pedestals created different feelings in the room, potential, loss, overecstatic, unguided change
- Solving the puzzle caused the feeling to change from "nothing matters" to "this is how things are but it could change" to much more hopeful
- The crystal represented a 5th element, like the uniqueness of the knowing races or fairy magic and mortal magic - magic controlled by will
- [[Clover]]'s footsteps lead deeper into the garden, to a set of 3 doors, with skies above
- Pass rare cuttings, one a cutting from the [[Royal Spire]], gift from [[Lakhyoto Tenua]]
- Scavenge a memory storing orb - more complex than we're familiar with
- Head through door past plaque saying past this point we'd be thrown out if not given permission by archdruid, king, or as visiting dignitary
- Reach a more thoroughly decorated garden with a fountain and giant untended tree, Clover's footprints end here
- Leads to very well-kept room, another massive tree, fainting couch, pedestal to accept the message orb, and a giant [[Tilly|construct]] focussed on pruning
- Letters on one wall "My dearest Love [[Amalenia]], from your king and husband, my druidess, a quiet place for you to rest"
